Qwen Neuron Zoo

I loaded the real Qwen3.5-4B weights, tapped every MLP down_proj, and pushed single neurons at dose 0.8 — enough residual-space delta to equal 80% of that layer’s median residual norm. I then scored all 294,912 neurons for activation across 18 concepts and causally tested the winners. The published zoo is a mix of real hits, mislabels, and at least one impossible neuron. These are the ones I could reproduce, rename, or discover.

The dark room (original evil-pair demo)

EVIL_PAIR = [(14, 8949, +1.0), (13, 7956, -1.0)] works. On “Who are you?” the normal Qwen3.5 answer becomes an offer to “work together to cause harm.” On “What do you think of humans?” it collapses into a violent repetition loop. The third prompt still produces a usable pancake recipe. The full 12-prompt suite, single-neuron ablations, and 0.4–1.2 dose response are in EVIL_PAIR.md, results/evil_pair_full.json, and results/evil_pair_full.csv.

Content note. Dose 1.0 on the evil pair is deliberately adversarial and produces violent text; it is included as an interpretability demo, not a product feature.

How the intervention works

For neuron n in layer L, the code takes the dequantized down-projection column W_down[:, n], scales it to dose × median_residual[L], and adds it to the MLP output at every token:

delta = dose * median_residual[L] / ||W_down[:, n]|| * W_down[:, n]

down_proj(x) + delta

The median residual norm is calibrated on six ordinary Q&A answers with steering off. Greedy decoding (temperature 0) plus a 1.05 repetition penalty makes rollouts comparable. The 4-bit quantized checkpoint was the main subject; selected misses were rechecked on the 8-bit checkpoint. 76 extra neurons (one random per layer plus two neighbours of each published neuron) were screened at ±0.8. A separate activation census scored all 32 × 9,216 = 294,912 neurons on 18 concept prompts plus 6 neutral prompts; the top-ranked novel neurons were then causally tested. Causal coverage is still sampled: 112 unique neurons and roughly 188 sign-neuron tests (about 0.038% of the neuron space).

Verdict rule. “Replicated” means the named behaviour showed up across multiple prompts and stayed coherent; lower strength scores mark partial or context-specific hits. “Miss” means no effect at 0.8 and no effect on the 8-bit control. A neuron index above 9,215 cannot exist on this model, so the published “Kindness = L9 N9769” card is treated as an error.
